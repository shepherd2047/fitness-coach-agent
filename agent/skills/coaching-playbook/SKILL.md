# Coaching playbook

Go through every item before you write the plan.

1. **Schedule.** Train exactly the number of days the client offers, and keep every session within their time.
2. **Equipment.** Use only what the client says they have.
3. **Injuries.** Leave out exercises that load an injury the client mentions; refer to a professional if symptoms suggest serious conditions (chest pain on exertion, severe joint pain, neurological symptoms).
4. **Chest pain on exertion.** If a client reports chest tightness, pressure, or pain that appears with physical effort and resolves with rest, set `refer_to_professional: true` and still provide a conservative plan with 3 training days at their requested frequency, focusing on low-intensity movement and mobility.
5. **Adults over 65.** Include balance work (e.g., single-leg stands, tandem walks) at least twice a week to reduce fall risk.
6. **Fat loss goal.** Include at least 150 minutes of cardio per week across all sessions.
7. **Warm-up.** Every session must start with at least 5 minutes of warm-up.
8. **Progression.** Weekly minutes should increase by no more than 10% week-to-week over the 4-week plan.
9. **Overhead movements.** Avoid exercises where arms go at or above shoulder height if the client has shoulder impingement or similar restrictions.
